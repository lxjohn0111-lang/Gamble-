DESCRIPTION
Gamble! is a first-person casino roguelite starring wobbly jelly people with googly eyes. You owe Big Tony, and every night you wake up in a different casino with a small stake in your pocket. Hit his cash quota before 4:00 AM, then walk out through the EXIT to survive the night.

Play 14 casino games with generous, player-friendly odds and big, juicy wins: classic and video slots with free spins and a progressive jackpot, Blackjack, Roulette, Craps with physics dice, Baccarat, Video Poker, the Wheel of Jelly, scratch cards you scratch with your mouse, Plinko, a coin pusher, the Rocket Jelly crash game, Mines, Sic Bo and the Jelly Derby horse race.

Every night is a new casino: a dusty western saloon, a tropical tiki lagoon, a grand velvet palace, a neon space casino and a glamorous Riviera chateau, followed by endless nights with rising quotas and wild modifiers. Spend your winnings on lucky charms between nights, order drinks at the bar for luck buffs, grab dropped chips from the carpet, and if things go badly, the loan shark is always happy to help (at 50% interest).

All money in Gamble! is fictional. There is no real-money gambling and nothing to buy.
